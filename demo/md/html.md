Donald Trump's business career is characterized by bold ventures and significant challenges. Initially leveraging family connections, he established a foothold in the real estate industry, rapidly expanding into diverse ventures. His brand became synonymous with high-profile properties, casinos, and lucrative licensing agreements [[c:S7-C2]]. In the **1980s**, Trump entered the casino business in **Atlantic City**, acquiring his first casino in **1984**, which he renamed Trump Plaza. The following year, he purchased the unfinished Hilton casino, renaming it Trump Castle. His largest casino, the Trump Taj Mahal, opened in **1990** at a cost of nearly **$1 billion**. Despite these ambitious projects, the casinos faced financial difficulties, leading to multiple bankruptcies in the **1990s** and early **2000s**. Trump managed to retain some ownership initially, but these ventures highlighted the financial volatility of his business empire [[c:S7-C2]][[c:S7-C1]].
